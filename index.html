How to play

  • Move with WASD / arrow keys (or drag on touch).

  • Aliens pour in from every edge. Don't let them touch you.
  • Kills drop green XP gems — grab them to level up and pick an upgrade.
  • Every 30s a new wave ramps up; watch for elites and bosses.
  • Press P to pause. Survive as long as you can!
